3 new blocks and 1 new UI component this week.

Motion and morphing ran through most of it — buttons that unfold into full surfaces.

## Blocks

**Check out Button.** A compact trigger that morphs into a checkout card with tabbed payment methods and a connect-wallet screen, all sharing one layout shell. It focuses on the button-to-card morph. Check it out here: [link]

**AI Chat.** A prompt composer where the plus button fans a radial attachment menu and the mic swaps to a live waveform mid-recording. It focuses on the radial attachment fan. Check it out here: [link]

**Theme Button.** A pill that opens into a picker so you can switch typeface and theme in place. It focuses on the shared-layout morph from pill to menu. Check it out here: [link]

## UI

**Alert Dialog.** A confirmation dialog on Base UI's accessible primitive, with seven variations that each enter a different way — scale, spring overshoot, CRT unroll, a parting curtain. It focuses on the range of entrance techniques. Check it out here: [link]
